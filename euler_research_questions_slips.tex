\noindent
\questioncard{slipblue}{\cardheight}{Changing Diagrams a Little 1:}{Can you add a fence segment, without changing the number of posts?}
\hfill
\questioncard{slipblue}{\cardheight}{Changing Diagrams a Little 2:}{Can you add a fence segment, without changing the number of safe regions?}

\vspace{4mm}

\noindent
\questioncard{slipblue}{\cardheight}{Changing Diagrams a Little 3:}{Can you add a fence segment while keeping both the number of posts and the number of safe regions the same?}
\hfill
\questioncard{slipblue}{\cardheight}{Changing Diagrams a Little 4:}{What happens to the counts if you only add a few posts to the fences, without changing their shape?}

\vspace{4mm}

\noindent
\questioncard{slipgreen}{\cardheight}{My Diagram Has Exactly Four Posts (Part 1):}{Start with 4 posts. What is the smallest number of fence segments it must have to be connected, and how many safe regions does this smallest diagram have?}
\hfill
\questioncard{slipgreen}{\cardheight}{My Diagram Has Exactly 4 Posts (Part 2):}{Start with 4 posts. How many segments will I need for at least one safe region? What about two safe regions?}

\vspace{4mm}

\noindent
\questioncard{sliprose}{\cardheight}{Posts Plus Regions (Part 1):}{Add a fourth column for the total number of posts and safe regions. Fill in that column for all the examples you made. \hfill
\questioncard{sliprose}{\cardheight}{Posts Plus Regions (Part 2):}{What can you say about the link between this total and the number of segments?}
