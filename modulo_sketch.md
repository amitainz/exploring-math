# Brainstorming Content for Tutorial on Periods and Modulo Arithmetic


## Astronomical Periods Govern our Time Perception
- earth revolves around the sun
- moon revolves around the earth
- earth spins around its axis


## Time measurement has all these periods

- 7 days in a week
- 24 hours in a day, or 12 hours on a 12-hour clock
- 60 minutes in an hour

some of it is not unlike the decimal system (the minutes are a 
convenient subdivision of the hour) just a little "funky"


## Artificial Periods
- for manipulating numbers in ciphers, it is useful to have A be next to Z, so you 
can make sense of a rule like "replace every letter with the letter that's twice after it"

## Rhythms in music are cyclical 

think of the 4-3 polyrhythm (see other tutorial) - to know "where we are" it is enough to remember
what "hour" we are (we can even draw the instructions on a clock)
