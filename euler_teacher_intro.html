When creating a design she follows three steps carefully, to make sure it is sound.

Don't let the story take over. The rules should be clear and established independently of the story, or things can get confusing quickly (e.g. if you say you must have the fences connected so you can electrify them, how does that make sense where there are no dinosaurs to fence in?) It's better if the kids treat drawing diagrams as a game with rules, with the story in the background.
